Why Diversity Matters

In Schools

Why It Matters

School isn't just about math and books — it's where we learn how to treat each other.

Making space for everyone

School is much better when people don't feel like they have to change who they are to fit in. Including others just means being decent: noticing when someone is sitting by themselves and making them feel welcome.

Growing up grounded

When you only hang out with people who think and act exactly like you, you miss out. Listening to classmates with different backgrounds and ideas helps us actually understand the world.
